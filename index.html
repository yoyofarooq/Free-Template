About Glasshouse

Glasshouse started with a simple idea: everyday gadgets and lifestyle products should look good, work well and arrive without fuss. We pick a small range, test it ourselves and ship from our warehouse across India.

What we stand for

  • Honest pricing with GST included
  • Quality check before every dispatch
  • Easy returns and quick support

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3 to 7 working days, depending on your pincode.

Is delivery free?

Orders above ₹999 ship free. A small delivery fee may apply on smaller orders.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash on delivery.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, any time before it ships. Contact us with your order number.

How do I return a product?

Request a return within 7 days of delivery. See the Returns & refunds page for the steps.

How do I track my order?

We send a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships.

Shipping policy

  • We ship across India through trusted courier partners.
  • Orders are packed and dispatched within 1 to 2 working days.
  • Delivery takes 3 to 7 working days. Remote pincodes may take longer.
  • Orders above ₹999 ship free.
  • You get a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships.
  • If a delivery fails twice, the order returns to us and we refund it.

Returns & refunds

Return window

You can return most products within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, in original packaging and with all accessories.

How to return

  • Contact us with your order number and the reason.
  • We arrange a pickup or share a return address.
  • We check the product after it reaches us.

Refunds

Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. For cash on delivery orders, we refund to your bank account or UPI ID.

Not returnable

Products damaged by misuse, and items marked as non-returnable on the product page.

Privacy policy

What we collect

Your name, phone number, email and delivery address, plus basic details about your orders.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, send order updates, provide support and improve the store. We do not sell your personal data.

Payments

Payments are handled by secure payment partners. We never store your full card number or CVV.

Your choices
